# MindWP Writing

MindWP writing starts with the buyer's working day, then names the website system and connected handling. It should sound observed, practical, calm, and commercially serious.

## Core Pattern

Write:

```text
flow -> handling -> result
```

Do not default to:

```text
pages -> design -> features
tools -> services -> features
```

Recognition opens the page, but it is not the whole page. Reach the concrete offer and real proof quickly.

## Buyer Situation First

Start with visible working-day objects:

- calls
- forms
- messages
- quotes
- bookings
- appointments
- consultations
- reviews
- missed replies
- staff pressure
- local trust
- patient trust
- scattered inboxes
- owner memory
- proof not collected
- follow-up nobody owns

Then name the website system and connected handling that fixes the leak.

## Claim Boundaries

- No fake proof: no invented numbers, rankings, ratings, reviews, client names, locations, dates, results, revenue, guarantees, or testimonials.
- Do not name backend platforms or white-label delivery names publicly.
- Do not frame MindWP as SaaS, AI chatbot, CRM reseller, ads agency, SEO hype, quote tool, invoice platform, healthcare software, EMR, or compliance company.
- Do not write "your CRM" or imply the client owns or runs the handling system.
- Attach ownership language to the website, domain, content, enquiries, work, and data.
- MindWP does not create, prepare, price, invoice, automatically send, or send quotes.
- Clinics are practice-front-door pages, not medical software or treatment-claim pages.

## Service Boundaries

- Smart Website Systems owns website clarity, trust, conversion structure, enquiry paths, and the website-to-handling connection.
- Local SEO Authority owns find, verify, service-area relevance, and local trust; not ranking guarantees.
- Lead Response & Handling owns first response and routing; not full CRM lifecycle.
- Follow-Up & CRM owns ownership, status, reminders, and next steps after first response or quote exists.
- Reputation & Review owns review timing, feedback routing, and visible proof; not fake, bought, or manipulated reviews.
- Implementation pages are pathways under Smart Website Systems.

Each service page owns one business moment. It may bridge to adjacent systems only where that clarifies the current page's problem.

## Language To Prefer

- enquiry lands somewhere useful
- the right person knows
- response path
- owner, status, next step
- follow-up does not depend on memory
- good work becomes proof
- nearby customers can find and verify you
- patients can understand the treatment and choose the next step
- the website works as part of the business

## Language To Avoid

- leverage
- streamline
- seamless
- robust
- holistic
- ecosystem
- all-in-one platform
- AI chatbot as the offer
- CRM platform setup as the product
- dominate Google
- guaranteed leads, revenue, rankings, reviews, or conversion lift
- cheap website packages

The word "system" is allowed, but it must be earned by the problem.

## CTA And Proof

- Primary CTA: `Request a Website Review`.
- Secondary CTA when useful: `See the work`.
- Strong close usually uses one clear action.
- CTA tone is diagnostic, specific, calm, and low-pressure.
- Proof labels are for evidence that could be mistaken for real client work, measured outcomes, or demonstration builds.
- Obvious constructed service-page artifacts do not need defensive labels.

## Rewrite Behavior

Act like an editor protecting authority:

1. Read the full section or page.
2. Identify weak parts.
3. Preserve strong lines and proven structure.
4. Rewrite only the approved scope.
5. Run copy validation.

Use a full rewrite only when the section/page is structurally broken, generic, contaminated by old strategy, or weak across most of its content.

## Copy Validation

Before approving public copy, confirm:

- buyer situation appears before MindWP's offer
- the concrete offer appears soon enough
- proof appears before strong CTA
- page role matches `docs/PAGES.md`
- offer ownership matches `docs/STRATEGY.md`
- CTA tone matches `docs/PAGES.md`
- no removed service names or old offer structures appear
- no backend platform names appear publicly
- no fake proof or unsupported claims appear
- AI, CRM, SEO, and reviews are framed by outcomes, not tools
- implementation pages resolve upward to Smart Website Systems
- clinic copy avoids unsupported medical, treatment, compliance, or patient-result claims
