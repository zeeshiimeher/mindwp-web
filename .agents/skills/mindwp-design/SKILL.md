# MindWP Design

MindWP should feel calm, premium, human, specific, credible, and properly made. Design has to make commercial meaning visible before it shows visual taste.

Use `docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` for practical CSS-system roles, typography roles, media handling, and rendered QA widths.

## Planning Questions

Before a major section is built, define:

- section job
- buyer-world idea or business moment
- buyer message
- composition and visual form
- hierarchy and dominance
- rhythm with adjacent sections
- proof or media need, if any
- mobile behavior
- fail conditions

Composition follows the buyer message. Do not choose a form because it appears in old guidance or in another page.

## Form Choice

A section may use text, media, screenshots, illustration, cards, lists, diagrams, strong typography, motion, or no supporting visual. Decide during page planning.

The question is whether the form makes the section clearer, more credible, and easier to act on. If the section needs too much surrounding explanation, revisit the section job, hierarchy, or copy before adding more surface detail.

## Coherence

A page should feel shaped by one deliberate system:

- consistent radius and elevation language
- consistent type scale and spacing rhythm
- controlled accent use
- related interaction states
- clear relationship between section density, emphasis, and page flow

Before adding a new visual language, decide what job the current language cannot do.

## Mobile Hierarchy

Mobile must preserve hierarchy, not merely stack desktop shapes. For each important section:

- keep the main point visible early
- keep proof or media readable when present
- avoid cramped labels and awkward headline wraps
- keep CTA and tap targets reachable
- keep contrast and focus states visible

If a composition risks breaking below 400px, report the risk instead of making 375px the primary design target.

## Rendered Audit

Audit rendered work with:

- first-glance commercial meaning
- section job clarity
- hierarchy and dominance
- rhythm and pacing
- repeated weak patterns
- proof or media readability when present
- typography wrapping
- desktop composition
- mobile hierarchy
- CTA clarity
- what must be revised
- what can remain

Use 1440px and 1280px desktop, 400px mobile, and 1024px tablet when nav, media, grids, multi-column sections, or major responsive structure change.
